After upload, reload the website. Updated CSS and JavaScript URLs include new cache versions. When testing locally, serve the folder over HTTP (for example, `python -m http.server 8000`); PDF font loading requires an HTTP/HTTPS origin.

## Changes

1. Removed the redundant info-icon circle; aligned icons and summaries; fixed subject-selector padding and wrapping; removed decorative card overlays; retained keyboard-focus outlines and input-validation cues.
2. Shared category detection across the website and record tools. Labels such as `STEM-EAD BOYS`, `STEM-EAD GIRLS`, `Grade 8 Boys`, and `Girls:` are dividers. Whole words are required; partial names such as Boysen and comma-separated personal names are not headings. Mixed `Boys/Girls` is deliberately not treated as a single category. Dividers have no learner number, no score entry, no Excel grade formulas, and are excluded from headcounts, completion checks, learner search and official grade exports. Separate blue and plum fills emphasize the two categories in light and dark themes.
3. Reduced score and learner text to 12 px, compacted score columns, and kept larger touch targets on coarse-pointer devices.
4. Excel exports contain two worksheets in order: Class Record and Activities. Both have actual OOXML Legal (8.5 × 14 inches), landscape, one-page-wide/one-page-tall settings and explicit print areas. Activity IDs match the score columns and include title, competency, topic, date and HPS.
5. Strengthened class-header and section-card colors and mapped each card to its actual selected accent.
6. Word exports use native editable paragraphs and tables. PDF exports use selectable text and vector lines with embedded fonts, rather than page screenshots. The official report retains its Letter-size format; the Legal-size requirement applies to Excel.
7. Fixed narrow-screen navigation/header collisions, Add Class dialog field overflow, dropdown width and tooltip placement. Shared styling also applies to the policy page.

## Print the Excel export

Choose **Print Entire Workbook** in Excel to print Class Record as page 1 and Activities as page 2. Select **Legal (8.5 × 14 inches)** paper in the printer driver as well. Each worksheet is fitted to one page; unusually large rosters, extra activity columns or very long activity descriptions necessarily reduce printed text size. These exports preserve the existing final-grade snapshot behavior; regenerate the workbook after editing scores in the website.

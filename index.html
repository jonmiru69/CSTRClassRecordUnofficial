Privacy and use

Technical notice updated 1 November 2026. School authorization and grading policy must be confirmed by the responsible school staff.

Operator and support

Developer contact: SANCHEZRAMIL2202@GMAIL.COM. Use this address for account approval, record recovery, correction, deletion, and privacy requests. Send only your sign-in email and a brief request. Never send passwords, account codes, learner lists, or grades through ordinary email. The developer must verify identity before granting access or disclosing records.

What the workspace stores

Teacher profile information, class rosters, assessment dates, scores, and calculated grades are stored in the configured Firebase Realtime Database. Firebase Authentication manages email/password or Google sign-in. Google receives the information needed to provide these services. Each approved account accesses its own workspace or a workspace assigned through a verified administrator mapping.

Recovery and shared computers

Local drafts and up to eight recovery checkpoints are stored in this browser tab's session storage. Logout clears current-account recovery. Browser session restoration may restore tab data, so always log out on a shared device. Theme and sidebar preferences can remain locally. Encrypted backups are protected with your passphrase; losing it prevents recovery. Downloaded spreadsheets, PDFs, Word documents and learner reports are not encrypted by this application. Store and share them carefully.

Android app and updates

The Android app keeps sign-in in its private app storage until you log out. Drafts and recovery checkpoints are encrypted in app-private storage using a non-exportable Android Keystore key, and cleared on logout. Device failure, app removal, or loss of that key can make local recovery unavailable; keep cloud saves or passphrase-protected backups. Exports pass through temporary app-private cache files into Android’s save or share sheet. Temporary exports are removed on the next launch. The app checks public release metadata on launch, resume and periodically while open. Optional Android background update alerts require notification permission and are scheduled by the operating system; they are not instant push delivery. Update checks do not send learner records or account credentials. Downloaded app updates must retain the same signing identity. Save your work before installing an update and keep the app installed when upgrading.

Saving and conflicts

Wait for successful cloud-save confirmation before changing devices. Saves based on an outdated cloud revision are rejected. Settings offers recovery checkpoints and a choice between whole versions, not an automatic merge. Back up both versions before replacement. Internet interruption and free-service limits can prevent cloud access.

Approval and older records

Creating an account does not grant access. The developer verifies and approves the account. Older records are reconnected by the developer after verifying ownership. Account codes are not accepted as proof of ownership or used as passwords.

Responsible use

Only enter learner data when authorized by the school. Collect only necessary information. Review grading weights, attendance-code treatment, transmutation, term structure and exported names against the school's approved policy. Computed results require teacher review. This release does not certify compliance with any school policy or law.

Retention and requests

The application does not automatically delete cloud records at the end of a school year. The developer and school must establish and publish the authorized retention period. Request correction, export, or deletion through the contact above. Identity verification and applicable school retention requirements apply. No response deadline is promised by this software.

Learner lookup

Lookup is inside the signed-in teacher workspace. It does not authenticate learners or parents. Verify the requester's identity and check class details when names match before displaying or sharing results.

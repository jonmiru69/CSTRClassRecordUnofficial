# CST-R and Class Record

Keep the existing CST-R class-record paper workspace, folder tabs and school seal unchanged. The neutral companion uses a flat paper-and-blue ledger style: strong publication-like headings, a single school seal, and a live synthetic sheet as the demonstration. Existing self-hosted Nimbus fonts provide the serif heading and sans-serif reading voices. Gold belongs to the supplied mark and controlled focus accents. No school campus or institutional emblems travel to the neutral product.

The entry uses two color fields and decisive typography from the selected composition. The workflow forms use direct labels, one primary action, native controls, and inline feedback. Navigation, keyboard focus, responsive tables and reduced-motion behavior take precedence over decorative transitions. Practice data is visibly synthetic in the editor and exports.

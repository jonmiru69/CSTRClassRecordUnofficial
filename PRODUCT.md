# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Philippine basic-education teachers recording assessments and preparing class reports; existing CST-R faculty continue in their institutional workspace. The Capacitor Android app retains its CST-R experience.

## Product Purpose

Make each grade traceable to the teacher's recorded evidence and preserve a usable record through offline work, concurrent saves, and report export. The neutral entry makes school identity and supported policy clear.

## Operating Context

Preserve active accounts, Auth UIDs, bindings, records, and backups in place. No billing account, credits, paid API, or learner data sent to external AI. School choice is not authorization. Owner and school must approve production changes and storage use independently.

## Brand Commitments

Use the supplied Class Record seal for the neutral site. Preserve CST-R visuals and outputs on the existing faculty path. Use plain wording and label supplementary and synthetic reports clearly.

## Evidence on Hand

The audit pins commit 226c2fb and documents workbook defects and unresolved approvals. The first neutral pilot uses only synthetic Grade 6 Science data. Do not claim official ECR parity or offer identifiable public-school storage until approved.
